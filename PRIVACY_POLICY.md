# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 2026

**App:** Meal or No Meal

## Data Collection

This application uses third-party advertising and in-app purchase services which may collect certain data:

### Advertising (Google AdMob)
- **What:** Device information, advertising ID, approximate location, app usage data
- **Why:** To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements
- **Who:** Google (AdMob)
- **Opt-out:** Users can purchase "Remove Ads" ($1.99) to disable all advertisements permanently

### In-App Purchases
- **What:** Purchase transaction data (handled by Google Play / Apple App Store)
- **Why:** To process and validate "Remove Ads" purchase
- **Who:** Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple StoreKit (iOS)
- **Storage:** Purchase status stored locally on device

## No Personal Data Collection by App Developer

The app developer (Nothical Nonsence) does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal user data.

## Third-Party Services

- Google Mobile Ads SDK (advertising)
- Google Play Billing Library / Apple StoreKit (payments)

## User Rights

- Purchase "Remove Ads" to stop all advertising
- Restore previous purchases on any device
