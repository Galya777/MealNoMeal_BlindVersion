# Store submission checklist

## Required links

- Privacy Policy URL (public)
- Support email

## Google Play

- App bundle (.aab)
- App signing configured in Play Console
- Screenshots (phone)
- App icon (512x512)
- Feature graphic (1024x500)
- Content rating questionnaire
- Data safety form (based on your real data collection)

## App Store

- Archive/IPA (signed)
- Screenshots (iPhone, optionally iPad)
- App icon set
- App privacy answers in App Store Connect
- Age rating

## Assets

- Ensure you have rights to all images/audio/fonts
